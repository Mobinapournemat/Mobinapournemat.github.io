%------------------------------- OVERVIEW -------------------------------------
\section{Overview}
\small I am a third-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, College Park,
advised by Prof.~Soheil Feizi. My research centers on reasoning in large language models --- in
particular \textbf{efficient inference and reasoning}, \textbf{reasoning faithfulness}, and the
\textbf{evaluation} of what these models can and cannot do reliably. My recent work includes a
benchmark for probabilistic reasoning under uncertainty (EMNLP 2026, with Meta AI), methods for
lowering the cost of long chain-of-thought reasoning, and a study of LLM answer consistency beyond
accuracy. I also have experience in multimodal generative models and computer vision, and prior
industry experience in applied machine learning and data science.
